{%- comment -%} Hero background for layouts with `hero: true`. Below 1023px the background renders at natural size from the top-left, so narrower screens get lossless top-left crops (hero-xs.jpg up to 480px, hero-small.jpg up to 1022px) that look identical. Preloading matters because a CSS background isn't discovered until the styles apply. The background color is the image's average, so the white hero text is readable before the image arrives. {%- endcomment -%}
