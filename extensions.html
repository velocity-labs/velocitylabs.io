How they protect your privacy

These extensions collect no data. They have no analytics, no accounts and no servers, and they're built so they can't send anything anywhere: every page they show blocks all network requests. Anything they read stays in your browser.

  • Only after you click. Installing one gives it no access to any website. It can see a page only after you click its toolbar button on that page, and only that tab.
  • "Always on" is one site at a time. If you want an extension to start by itself on a site you use a lot, you can turn that on in its panel. Your browser asks you to confirm, naming the site, and it covers that site only.
  • Settings stay in your browser. The few choices they remember, like your export format, are kept in the extension's own storage, which websites can't read.
