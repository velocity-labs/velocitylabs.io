Built with FastAPI on the HUD User Data API. - name: paycheck kind: Web app · Node.js url: https://paycheck.velocitylabs.io logo: /images/tools/paycheck.svg technologies: [Node.js, TypeScript, API Integration, Docker] text: >- Shows what a job pays where you live. Pick one of over 800 jobs and a state or metro area, and paycheck shows the median and the spread of wages, how they compare with the rest of the country, and where the job pays most. Add your own pay to see where it falls, or start from the wage you want and see which local jobs pay it. Built with Express and TypeScript on the Bureau of Labor Statistics' wage data. ---
