Privacy

{{ ext.name }} collects no data. It has no analytics, no accounts and no servers, and its pages block every network request (a Content Security Policy of connect-src 'none'), so it can't send anything anywhere. Anything it reads stays in your browser.

  • Only after you click. Installing it gives it no access to any website. It can see a page only after you click its toolbar button on that page, and only that tab.

  • "Always on" is one site at a time. If you turn it on, your browser asks you to confirm, naming the site, and it covers that site only. Nothing is granted at install, and you can turn it off from the panel or in your browser's extension settings.
  • Nothing hidden. In Chrome and Edge, the "Always on" confirmation is a page websites can load, so a site could tell that {{ ext.name }} is installed, though its address changes every session to make that hard. It reveals nothing else. In Firefox and Safari the confirmation opens in a small window instead, and no site can load any part of it.
